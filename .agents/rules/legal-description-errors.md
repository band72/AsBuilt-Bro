# BoundaryQC AI Implementation Skills & Heuristics

This file documents the specialized capabilities and algorithms added to the BoundaryQC AI Agent system for tracing, extracting, and verifying land boundary geometry from survey plats and legal descriptions.

## 1. Topological Sequence Checking (Out-of-Order Prevention)
**Problem:** The AI vision models sometimes extract call-outs in random locations or out of the correct sequential polygon order around the image.
**Solution:** A strict instruction was added to the AI extraction prompt forcing it to physically re-trace the perimeter with its extracted bearing/distance numbers before formatting the JSON. It must mathematically verify that `Segment 1 Endpoint` geometrically touches the `Segment 2 Startpoint`. If its internal traversal path jumps across the page or points backwards, the AI self-identifies that the data is extracted sequentially out-of-order and must reorder the array.

## 2. Self-Intersecting Line Prevention (Bow-tie Check)
**Problem:** If the sequence is out of order, or if the N/S E/W bearing quadrant is accidentally flipped (a very common transcription error in surveying), the constructed geometry crosses over itself and forms a "bow-tie" shape.
**Solution:** The AI prompt forces the agent to physically project its parsed coordinate geometry before returning. Since single-family property boundaries can legally *never* cross their own path, the AI uses this self-intersecting line constraint as a heuristic. If it detects crossing coordinate lines, it knows it must flip a bearing quadrant or reorder the segments until the interior polygon lines do not cross.

## 3. Left vs Right Curve Direction
**Problem:** A `Left` vs `Right` curve designation is required to mathematically recreate the survey arc (Concave vs Convex geometry).
**Solution:** Using a spatial analogy, the prompt instructs the AI to imagine driving a car clockwise down the straight tangential chord bearing. If the physical arc bows OUT to the LEFT of the vehicle (away from the center of the referenced lot), it is a Left curve. If it bows OUT to the RIGHT (bulging inwards towards the center of the lot), it is a Right curve.

## 4. Native Plat-To-DXF Agent Command
**Problem:** The `AI Agent Chat` did not natively output all the auxiliary files generated by the dedicated `Plat to DXF` extraction buttons.
**Solution:** The Agent`s message pump was modernized to intercept `.jpg` or `.png` attachments. Instead of processing them as standard text questions, it invokes the heavyweight `ExtractPlatCallsFromImageAsync` engine. This triggers the V3 structural pattern recognition, forces the topology to close mathematically to the Point of Beginning, and automatically drops the `.dxf`, Coordinate `.txt` points list, and Agent Extracted COGO Scripts natively into the exact local folder where the user's uploaded image was located.

## 5. Moore-Neighbor Tracing Algorithm
**Problem:** Previous Skeletonization/Thinning algorithms (Zhang-Suen) dropped corners on "T-intersections" or ink-bleeding areas of images.
**Solution:** The `ImageIsolator` engine uses the `Moore-Neighbor` contour perimeter boundary tracing algorithm. This traces the precise outer perimeter of the highlighted mask, ignoring internal noise and guaranteeing that nodes (red dots) are painted strictly on the outer tangent vertices, providing the AI with a perfectly outlined target path.

**Future Development Notes:** Ensure any additional computer-vision checks continue to utilize the "Red Dot" skeletal trace nodes, as they represent the mathematically isolated ground-truth of the boundary line, completely separate from the OCR text readouts.
